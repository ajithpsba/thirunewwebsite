Corporate Governance &
Outside General Counsel

Ongoing legal counsel for company decisions, records, and operations.

Thiru Legal PLLC advises privately held companies, founders, and family businesses on how they make decisions, document approvals, and manage changes in ownership or leadership. We help clarify who can act for the company, which decisions need approval, and what records to keep. We also serve as outside general counsel for businesses that need ongoing legal advice as questions arise.

Governance Documents

We prepare and update the documents that govern how your company is owned and managed. We review how decisions are made in practice and help put those procedures in writing.

  • Governing agreements. Draft and update LLC operating agreements, corporate bylaws, and shareholder agreements.
  • Roles and decision-making. Define the responsibilities of directors, officers, managers, and owners. Clarify voting rights and which decisions require board or owner approval.
  • Signing authority. Document who can sign contracts, commit company funds, or delegate authority.
  • Governance policies. Establish procedures for handling conflicts of interest and transactions involving owners, managers, or affiliated companies.

Board & Owner Approvals

We help companies identify who needs to approve a proposed action and prepare a written record of the decision.

  • Meetings and written consents. Prepare notices, agendas, minutes, resolutions, and written consents for boards, shareholders, LLC members, and managers.
  • Approval procedures. Review notice, quorum, and voting requirements under the company's governing documents.
  • Significant company actions. Document approvals for major contracts, loans, distributions, and other transactions. For example, when a company plans to borrow money, we can review its governing documents, identify the approvals needed, and prepare the resolutions authorizing the loan and the person who will sign it.

Corporate Records & Compliance

A company's records can fall behind as the business grows. A new owner may have joined without a corresponding update to the ownership records, or a management change may have left signing authority unclear. We review the records, identify discrepancies, and advise on how to address them.

  • Governance reviews. Compare governing documents, ownership records, and past approvals to identify inconsistencies, missing records, or actions that may need follow-up.
  • Company records. Organize minutes, resolutions, written consents, and membership or shareholder records. Help management establish a routine for documenting significant decisions.
  • Filing schedules. Identify recurring company filings and assist with updates to registered agent, office, or other company information where applicable.
  • Records for transactions. Help organize records requested by a lender, investor, or prospective buyer before financing, a sale, or an ownership transfer.

Ownership & Leadership Changes

We help companies document changes in ownership or management, including succession in a family business. We address who will own the business and who will run it, since those responsibilities may pass to different people or change at different times.

  • Ownership changes. Review transfer restrictions and approval requirements. Document the admission of a new owner, issuance or transfer of interests, or an owner buyout.
  • Leadership changes. Document appointments, resignations, removals, and changes in responsibilities. Update signing authority and company records to reflect the transition.
  • Family business succession. Help plan who will take over management if an owner retires, becomes incapacitated, dies, or leaves the business.
  • Buy-sell provisions. Draft or update terms that address when a buyout may occur, how the interest will be valued, and how payment will be made.
  • Estate planning coordination. Review how ownership transfer restrictions fit with proposed transfers to family members or trusts, and coordinate company agreements with the owners' wills, trusts, and powers of attorney.

Governance for Related Companies

When parent companies, subsidiaries, or affiliates share owners or managers, we help clarify which entity is making a decision, signing a contract, or taking on an obligation.

  • Authority across entities. Review ownership, management, and signing authority for each company involved.
  • Entity-specific approvals. Prepare separate resolutions, consents, and other records for each entity when several companies participate in a transaction.
  • Intercompany arrangements. Help document shared services, use of assets, and allocation of costs where appropriate.
  • Expansion and reorganization. Coordinate governance documents and approvals when the group adds an entity, reorganizes, or expands into the United States.

Outside General Counsel

We provide a regular point of contact for owners and managers who need legal guidance on day-to-day operations and significant business decisions. We can assist with a specific project or through an ongoing engagement with an agreed scope.

  • Review recurring customer, vendor, and service agreements and advise on proposed changes.
  • Discuss the legal implications of a proposed business decision before management commits to it.
  • Help management identify and document approvals for major contracts, financing, distributions, and other transactions.
  • Advise on conflicts of interest, related-party transactions, and the responsibilities of directors, officers, and managers.
  • Assess developing business disputes and discuss options for responding or negotiating a resolution.
  • Coordinate with your accountant, other advisers, or specialized counsel when a matter calls for their input.
