Thiru Legal offers several convenient payment options to accommodate our clients' preferences. Follow the payment instructions provided on your invoice or directly by our office. Each option allows you to make payments through an established payment service or your bank.

Payment Methods

  • Card or eCheck: Pay by credit card, debit card, or eCheck through our designated payment link. An eCheck is an electronic payment drawn directly from your bank account.
  • Zelle or Venmo: Zelle and Venmo allow you to send payments electronically using their supported banking or mobile applications. Confirm the recipient details with our office before making your payment.
  • Wire Transfer: A wire transfer sends funds directly from your bank to the designated receiving account. Contact our office for wire instructions and verify the account details with us before initiating the transfer.
  • PayPal: PayPal allows you to make an electronic payment through your PayPal account or other payment options available through the platform. Use only the payment link or recipient details provided directly by our office.

Payment Security

These payment channels provide security features to help protect transactions. Before paying, verify that you are using the correct payment link and sending funds to the recipient confirmed by our office.

Thiru Legal does not authorize third parties to solicit money on our behalf. Although we use payment platforms to process transactions, payment requests and instructions come directly from our office.

If you receive an unexpected payment request, different account information, or changed payment instructions, call 832-500-5171 to verify the details before sending funds.

Protecting Your Information

Please discuss with our office before sending confidential documents or sensitive personal or financial information. We will explain what information is needed and how to provide it securely. Enter card and banking details directly through the designated payment platform.
