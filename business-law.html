Business Law & Contracts

Practical legal counsel for business owners and entrepreneurs.

Thiru Legal PLLC helps business owners form companies, negotiate contracts, lease commercial space, and buy or sell businesses. Based in Houston, we work with entrepreneurs, privately held companies, and international businesses entering the U.S. market.

Business Formation

We help you choose a business structure and prepare the documents needed to get started. We discuss who will own and manage the business, what each owner will contribute, and how key decisions will be made.

  • Entity selection and formation. Evaluate LLCs, corporations, and partnerships based on your ownership and operating plans. Prepare the applicable formation documents and filings.
  • Founder and ownership arrangements. Document ownership percentages, capital contributions, management responsibilities, and voting rights.
  • Initial company documents. Prepare LLC operating agreements, corporate bylaws, and organizational approvals appropriate to the entity.
  • Registration for existing businesses. Assess whether an entity formed elsewhere needs to register where it plans to operate. Assist with applicable registrations, assumed-name filings, and amendments.
  • Ownership changes. Document the admission of new owners or investors, ownership transfers, buyouts, and owner departures. Review existing restrictions on transfers and provisions governing valuation and buyout rights.
  • Restructuring and winding down. Assist with select entity changes, reorganizations, and business closures.

Contract Drafting & Review

A contract should make clear what each party must deliver, when payment is due, and what happens if the agreement ends or a party fails to perform. We draft agreements for new arrangements, review contracts you receive, and negotiate terms based on your priorities.

  • Customer, vendor, and supplier agreements. Define deliverables, payment terms, deadlines, acceptance procedures, and each party's responsibilities.
  • Consulting and independent contractor agreements. Document the scope of work, compensation, confidentiality, and ownership of work product.
  • Sales, equipment, distribution, and dealership agreements. Review pricing, delivery, warranties, sales territories, and termination rights.
  • Confidentiality and licensing agreements. Address the use and disclosure of confidential information, software licensing, access to source code, and intellectual property ownership.
  • Risk allocation. Review indemnification, limits on liability, insurance requirements, and dispute resolution provisions.
  • Amendments, renewals, and terminations. Document changes to existing agreements and prepare termination notices, releases, or transition agreements where appropriate.

Buying & Selling a Business

We assist buyers and sellers from the letter of intent through closing. We help identify what is being transferred, what liabilities each side will assume, and which issues need to be resolved before the deal closes.

  • Letters of intent and deal terms. Review the proposed price, payment structure, exclusivity, confidentiality, due diligence period, and which provisions the parties intend to make binding.
  • Transaction structure. Evaluate an asset purchase, stock purchase, or LLC membership interest purchase in coordination with your financial and tax advisers as appropriate.
  • Legal due diligence. Review ownership records, key contracts, leases, disclosed liabilities, and required third-party consents.
  • Purchase agreements. Draft and negotiate terms covering what transfers, which liabilities the buyer assumes, representations and warranties, indemnification, and conditions to closing.
  • Payment and closing arrangements. Document agreed payment terms, including seller financing, escrow, or holdbacks where appropriate. Prepare assignments, approvals, and other closing documents.
  • Post-closing transition. Document handover responsibilities and prepare transition services or consulting agreements where needed.

Commercial Leasing

We draft, review, and negotiate commercial leases for office, retail, warehouse, and industrial space. Whether you're signing a new lease, renewing, or taking over an existing location as part of a business acquisition, we help you understand the costs and obligations before you commit.

  • Lease terms and costs. Review base rent, rent increases, deposits, operating expenses, and common area maintenance charges.
  • Use and build-out. Review permitted use, the condition in which the landlord will deliver the space, and responsibility for improvements, repairs, and maintenance.
  • Guarantees and risk allocation. Review personal guarantees, insurance requirements, indemnity provisions, and default terms.
  • Renewals and changes. Negotiate renewal options, amendments, assignments, subleases, and landlord consents.
  • Exit arrangements. Review termination rights and negotiate surrender agreements, including move-out obligations and any remaining payment responsibilities.

Construction & Contractor Agreements

We draft and review agreements for property owners, contractors, and subcontractors. We help define what the project includes, when payment is due, and how changes will be handled once work begins.

  • Scope and schedule. Specify the work, materials, project milestones, and each party's responsibilities.
  • Payment terms. Document deposits, progress payments, and conditions for final payment.
  • Change orders. Establish how changes to the work, price, or schedule will be approved and recorded.
  • Delays, warranties, and termination. Review provisions for project delays, correction of deficient work, and ending the agreement.

Contract Disputes & Settlement

When an agreement breaks down, we review the contract, correspondence, and supporting records to help you assess your position and decide how to respond.

  • Early dispute assessment. Review disagreements over unpaid invoices, incomplete work, missed deadlines, termination, or competing interpretations of an agreement.
  • Demand letters and breach notices. Prepare or respond to written demands. Review contract requirements for giving notice and any time allowed to correct a breach.
  • Settlement negotiations. Help you evaluate proposed resolutions and negotiate payment arrangements or other terms.
  • Settlement documents. Prepare agreements, releases, and payment plans that reflect what the parties agree to.

Business Transactions & Immigration

If a U.S. business purchase, investment, or expansion is part of your immigration plans, we can review the transaction alongside the immigration strategy. This may be relevant if you're considering an E-2 investment or opening a U.S. office and transferring key personnel.

  • Review proposed ownership and control before the parties finalize the company structure or acquisition.
  • Coordinate formation documents, purchase agreements, commercial leases, and business records with the immigration filings.
  • Review the timing of funding, signing, closing, and immigration filings so you can make informed commitments.
  • Help document the relationship between foreign and U.S. entities and the proposed roles of key personnel.
