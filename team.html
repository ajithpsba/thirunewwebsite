Thiru Sethuraman, JD, MBA, MS, CPA

Founder and Managing Attorney

Admitted in Texas and Indiana

Thiru Sethuraman brings 29 years of global experience in legal, regulatory, corporate compliance, and consulting roles, including 18 years of corporate experience at Cummins. His international work has taken him across the United States, Europe, China, India, Latin America, Southeast Asia, Australia, Japan, South Korea, and other markets. His background includes extensive work in data privacy, anti-money laundering, anti-corruption, contracts, corporate compliance, and investigations. He founded Thiru Legal to help individuals, families, and businesses navigate U.S. immigration, business law and contracts, corporate governance, and estate planning.

In his immigration practice, Thiru assists families with green cards and citizenship, represents clients in Immigration Court, and advises employers, entrepreneurs, and investors on visas and permanent residence. His business immigration work includes intracompany transfers, investor visas, employer sponsorship, PERM labor certification, and Form I-9 compliance. His experience working across cultures and business environments helps him understand the practical concerns of international clients.

Thiru’s corporate experience and accounting background inform his approach to advising business owners. He helps clients evaluate and negotiate contracts, document ownership and management decisions, address governance questions, and plan for business succession. His estate planning work includes wills, trusts, and arrangements designed to support clients’ personal, family, and business goals.

Thiru earned his JD from Indiana University, an MBA in Finance and Accounting, and an MS in Industrial Technology from Purdue University. He is also a Certified Public Accountant (CPA) and holds the Certified Estate Planner (CEP) designation from the National Institute of Certified Estate Planners. Originally from Malaysia, he brings firsthand experience of immigrating to the United States to his work with clients navigating that process.

thiru.sethuraman@thirulegal.com

Reginald Fox, JD

Of Counsel Admitted in Texas

Admitted in Texas

Reginald “Reggie” Fox brings three decades of experience representing clients in housing and consumer matters. A 1992 graduate of the Thurgood Marshall School of Law at Texas Southern University, he spent 30 years in the Housing and Consumer Division of Lone Star Legal Aid, handling a broad range of landlord–tenant disputes. His experience includes evictions, security deposits, rental repairs, lockouts, utility interruptions, and lease terminations.

Reggie also has extensive experience in consumer and property matters, including wrongful vehicle repossessions, judicial and nonjudicial foreclosure defense, unsatisfactory auto repairs, mechanic’s liens, home improvement contracts, and tow hearings. At Thiru Legal, he assists with select housing, consumer, real property, and small claims matters while preparing to represent clients seeking asylum.

In 2018, the Harris County Commissioners Court appointed Reggie to fill a vacancy as Justice of the Peace for Precinct 7, Place 1. During a leave of absence from Lone Star Legal Aid, he presided over eviction suits, consumer disputes, and traffic cases. He returned to legal aid practice after the newly elected judge took office.

Following his retirement from Lone Star Legal Aid at the end of 2023, Reggie completed mediation training through the Dispute Resolution Center of Harris County, where he volunteers as a mediator. He brings his experience as an advocate, former judge, and mediator to helping clients understand their options and work toward practical resolutions

Valentina Diaz, BBA

Senior Legal Assistant

Valentina Diaz has supported Thiru Legal’s clients and attorneys for several years, helping coordinate communications, collect documents, and organize case materials. As Senior Legal Assistant, she guides clients through the firm’s administrative processes, explains what information is needed, and follows up on outstanding items as their matters progress.

Valentina assists with consultation arrangements, intake, document preparation, and maintaining organized case files under attorney supervision. Her familiarity with the firm’s processes helps keep information moving between clients and attorneys. She understands that legal matters can feel unfamiliar and stressful, and she approaches client questions with patience, care, and attention to detail.

Her professional background includes customer service, bilingual communication, client relations, and team coordination. These skills help her work with clients from different backgrounds and support the firm’s daily operations. She earned her Bachelor of Business Administration in May 2025, building on her practical experience in organization, communication, and administrative support.

Sarasvathi Neelamegam, LLB

Legal Consultant

Sara Nelamegam brings nearly 20 years of professional experience spanning law and education. She holds a Bachelor of Laws (LLB) from the University of London and a certificate in Teaching English as a Second Language. Her background combines legal research and drafting with strong editorial and communication skills.

Sara has worked in various law offices, supporting legal analysis, client communications, the preparation of complex briefs, and the review of court documents. She also served as a legal editor at Thomson Reuters, further developing her attention to legal language, accuracy, and clarity. At Thiru Legal, she supports the firm’s attorneys with research, document preparation, and case organization.

Before transitioning into law, Sara taught English at private and international schools in Malaysia and served as Head of the English Department at The Learning Hub. Her teaching experience informs her patient, structured approach to helping clients understand information and prepare the materials needed for their matters.

Providing expert legal guidance in Immigration Law, Business Law, and Estate Planning. Dedicated to protecting your family, assets, and business interests.
