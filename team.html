Thiru Sethuraman, JD, MBA, MS, CPA

Founder and Managing Attorney

Admitted in Texas and Indiana

Thiru founded Thiru Legal to advise individuals, families, and businesses on decisions that affect where they live, how they operate, and how they plan for the future. His practice includes U.S. immigration, business law and contracts, corporate governance, and estate planning.

In his immigration practice, Thiru assists families with green cards and citizenship, represents clients in Immigration Court, and advises employers, entrepreneurs, and investors on visas and permanent residence. His business immigration work includes intracompany transfers, investor visas, employer sponsorship, PERM labor certification, and Form I-9 compliance.

Thiru's background in corporate compliance, investigations, mergers and acquisitions, and accounting informs his work with business owners. He helps clients evaluate contract terms, document ownership and management decisions, and coordinate business succession with their estate plans.

Thiru earned his JD from Indiana University, an MBA in Finance and Accounting, and an MS in Industrial Technology from Purdue University. He is also a Certified Public Accountant. Originally from Malaysia, Thiru brings firsthand experience of immigrating to the United States to his work with clients navigating that process.
