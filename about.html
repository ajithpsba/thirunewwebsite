About the Firm

A Houston-based law firm founded by Thiru Sethuraman.

Request a Consultation
Thiru Legal began as a U.S. immigration practice and now also advises on business law, corporate governance, and estate planning.

Thiru Legal PLLC is a Houston-based law firm founded by Thiru Sethuraman. We began as a U.S. immigration practice serving individuals, families, workers, and employers. Today, we also advise clients on business law and contracts, corporate governance, and estate planning, including wills and trusts. We serve as outside general counsel for businesses that need ongoing legal advice. We offer mediation and assist with select landlord and tenant, consumer, real property, and other civil and administrative matters.

Thiru's background in law, accounting, estate planning and business shapes how we approach each matter. We look at what a legal decision means in practice, whether it affects a family's immigration plans, the day-to-day operations of a business, or the people who will carry out an estate plan.

Our Mission

Help individuals, families, and businesses make informed legal decisions.

We listen to what you want to accomplish, explain your options in plain language, and help you choose a course of action that fits your circumstances.

Our Vision

Build lasting relationships as clients' needs change.

We aim to build lasting relationships with clients who can turn to us as their needs change. We want clients to feel comfortable seeking advice early, whether they are planning a move to the United States, starting or growing a business, or making arrangements for the people who depend on them.

Our Values

Respect, candor, practical judgment, and attention to detail.

  • Respect. We take the time to understand your priorities, your circumstances, and the concerns that brought you to us.
  • Candor. We explain the strengths, risks, and uncertainties of your matter so you can weigh your choices.
  • Practical judgment. We consider your goals, timing, and costs when recommending a next step.
  • Attention to detail. We pay close attention to the facts, documents, and deadlines that can affect your matter.

Our Promise

Explain the scope and fees, keep you informed, and walk you through your options.

When you work with Thiru Legal, we'll explain what our work together will cover, how fees will be charged, and what we need from you. We'll keep you informed of important developments and explain what they mean for your matter. When you need to make a decision, we'll walk you through the options and trade-offs so you can decide how to proceed.

How We Work With You

We start with your goals, what has happened, and any upcoming deadlines.

We start by asking what you want to accomplish, what has happened so far, and whether any deadlines are approaching. Depending on the matter, we may ask to review an immigration notice, a business agreement, or an existing will or trust. From there, we discuss the immediate decisions you face and whether we can help with a specific task or provide ongoing legal support.
