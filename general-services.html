General Services

Mediation, select civil matters, and help with agency and document requests.

Thiru Legal PLLC helps individuals and businesses resolve disputes, respond to government agencies, request records, and prepare documents for official use. Tell us what you need help with and mention any upcoming hearing, response deadline, or travel date.

Mediation

Mediation gives parties a structured setting to discuss a dispute with a neutral mediator. The mediator helps the parties identify the issues, consider possible solutions, and decide whether they can reach an agreement.

  • Family mediation. Parenting, property, and other family disagreements.
  • Business mediation. Disputes among owners, business partners, customers, vendors, or contractors.
  • Property and civil mediation. Rental disputes, neighbor disagreements, property damage, and other civil matters.
  • Workplace mediation. Select disagreements involving employees, managers, or workplace relationships.

If you are requesting mediation, briefly describe the dispute and mention any upcoming court date. Our team will follow up about the matter and discuss arrangements for the session.

Landlord–Tenant & Other Civil Matters

We assist with select rental, consumer, real property, and small claims matters. We can review your documents, explain your options, and discuss whether we can assist with correspondence, settlement, hearing preparation, or representation.

  • Landlord–tenant disputes. Evictions, security deposits, repair and remedy matters, lockouts, utility interruptions, and lease termination.
  • Consumer matters. Vehicle repossession, auto repair disputes, tow hearings, and select home repair and contractor disputes.
  • Real property matters. Title and boundary disputes, easements and property access, Transfer on Death Deeds, and judicial foreclosure defense.
  • Small claims and other civil disputes. Unpaid invoices, property damage, and contract disputes. We can help assess a claim or defense, prepare or respond to a demand letter, and explore settlement.

Government Agency Representation

We represent individuals and businesses in select matters before government agencies. We can review a notice or decision, identify what the agency is asking for, prepare a written response, and communicate with the agency on your behalf where appropriate.

  • Agency notices and inquiries. Review the notice, identify any stated deadline, and help gather the information or evidence the agency requests.
  • Written submissions. Prepare responses, explanations, and supporting materials for agency review.
  • IRS Offers in Compromise. Review your circumstances to assess whether an offer may be appropriate, help organize the financial records, and assist with preparing the submission and responding to IRS requests.
  • Agency decisions and follow-up. Review the decision and discuss available options for clarification, reconsideration, or further review.

For immigration-specific agency matters, see U.S. Immigration Law.

FOIA Requests

We help individuals and businesses request federal agency records under the Freedom of Information Act (FOIA). We can identify the agency likely to hold the records, prepare the request, and review the materials released.

  • Request preparation. Describe the records sought, identify relevant dates, and help prepare any authorization needed to request records on someone's behalf.
  • Agency follow-up. Respond to requests for clarification and make status inquiries when appropriate.
  • Review of released records. Organize the records, identify gaps or inconsistencies, and discuss how the information may relate to your matter.

For USCIS, CBP, ICE, or Immigration Court records, see Immigration Records & FOIA Requests on the U.S. Immigration Law page.

Apostille Requests

We help clients prepare documents for use abroad and request an apostille or other authentication where appropriate. We start by reviewing the document, where it was issued, and where you intend to use it.

  • Document review. Review vital records, diplomas, notarized documents, and business records.
  • Preparation. Help determine whether you need a certified copy or other supporting paperwork.
  • Submission assistance. Help identify the appropriate authority and prepare the request forms and supporting documents.

Passport Application Assistance

We provide administrative support for U.S. passport applications and renewals. We can help you identify the applicable forms and organize your supporting documents before submission.

  • First-time applications and renewals. Identify the applicable form and prepare a checklist of supporting documents.
  • Applications for children. Help organize documents relating to parentage, identification, and consent, as applicable.
  • Replacements and name changes. Help identify the paperwork for a lost or damaged passport or for an application in a new name.
