Privacy Policy

Thiru Legal PLLC
Effective Date: December 1, 2026

Thiru Legal PLLC (“Thiru Legal,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information through www.thirulegal.com, our online forms, and related communications.

Our handling of client information is also governed by applicable law, professional confidentiality obligations, and the terms of our engagement.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you request a consultation, contact our office, inquire about services, or make a payment. This may include:

  • Your name, email address, telephone number, and communication preferences.
  • The type of assistance requested and a brief description of your matter.
  • Your preferred consultation date, time, time zone, and meeting format.
  • Information provided through emails, calls, text messages, WhatsApp, or other communications.
  • Billing information and payment transaction details.

When necessary to evaluate or handle a matter, we may request additional information, including identification documents, immigration records, business records, financial information, or family information. Please contact our office before submitting sensitive documents.

Website and Technical Information

Our website and its service providers may collect technical information, including your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring website, and visit dates and times. Cookies, server logs, and similar technologies may support this collection.

2. How We Use Information

We use information as appropriate to:

  • Respond to inquiries and requests for assistance.
  • Conduct conflict checks and evaluate whether we can assist.
  • Arrange consultations and communicate about availability.
  • Provide requested or authorized services.
  • Manage client communications, billing, and payments.
  • Support research, document preparation, review, and case administration.
  • Operate, secure, maintain, and improve our website and systems.
  • Maintain records and meet legal and professional obligations.
  • Detect fraud, address security incidents, and protect legal rights.

3. Consultation Requests

Consultation requests submitted through our website are directed to info@thirulegal.com and may be reviewed by authorized personnel assisting with intake and scheduling.

A requested date or time is a preference, not a confirmed appointment. Our office will contact you to discuss availability, applicable fees, and arrangements.

Submitting a form or contacting us does not, by itself, establish an attorney–client relationship or confirm that we have accepted your matter. Duties concerning prospective-client information remain governed by applicable law and professional rules.

Please provide only a brief description in an initial inquiry. Do not include Social Security numbers, passport numbers, banking credentials, or sensitive documents in a general website form.

4. Firm Personnel and Service Providers

Thiru Legal works with attorneys, staff, consultants, and service providers located in the United States and overseas. Authorized personnel may access your contact information, documents, and matter-related information as reasonably necessary to support client communications, research, document preparation, case administration, and other firm operations.

Access is subject to appropriate supervision, confidentiality obligations, and safeguards consistent with the work performed and the information involved.

We also use service providers supporting website hosting, email, intake, document storage, communications, security, billing, payment processing, and other operational functions.

5. Technology-Assisted Work

We use technology, including artificial intelligence-assisted tools, to support research, drafting, document review, organization, and administrative tasks. Information relevant to these tasks may be processed through these tools, subject to applicable confidentiality obligations and appropriate safeguards.

6. Other Disclosures of Information

In addition to the access and processing described above, we may disclose information as reasonably necessary to:

  • Professionals and other parties involved in your matter, as authorized or otherwise permitted by applicable law and professional rules.
  • Courts, government agencies, or other recipients when disclosure is authorized, legally required, or otherwise permitted.
  • Parties necessary to address fraud, security incidents, or legal claims, subject to applicable confidentiality obligations.

The information disclosed depends on the purpose and may include contact information, inquiry details, matter-related records, technical information, or payment records.

7. Payment Information

Payment options may include credit or debit cards, eChecks, Zelle, Venmo, wire transfers, and PayPal. Payment providers and financial institutions process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

We may receive payer information, transaction references, payment confirmations, and other records needed for billing and reconciliation.

Enter payment credentials only through the designated payment service or your financial institution. Do not send full card numbers, passwords, or online banking credentials through website forms, email, or text messages.

Confirm payment instructions with our office before sending funds, especially if you receive an unexpected request or changed account details.

8. Cookies and Third-Party Features

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies for functionality, security, preferences, and website measurement.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings and any privacy controls available on the website. Blocking certain cookies may affect website features.

Embedded maps, external payment services, and other third-party features may collect information when loaded or used. Their privacy policies govern their processing of that information.

9. Communications and Your Preferences

We may respond using the contact information you provide, including by telephone, email, text message, or WhatsApp.

You may ask us to use a particular communication method or stop optional communications. Necessary communications concerning an active engagement may continue through an appropriate channel. Standard messaging and data charges may apply.

Tell us if your email address or telephone number is shared or is unsuitable for communications about your matter.

10. Information Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards appropriate to the information we handle. No electronic transmission or storage system is completely secure.

Contact our office before sending sensitive information so we can explain an appropriate submission method.

11. Information Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain conflict and client records, resolve disputes, and satisfy legal and professional obligations.

Retention periods vary by information type and circumstances. Certain records may be retained after an inquiry or engagement ends.

You are responsible for keeping your own copies of documents and information you provide to us or receive from us, including correspondence, filings, notices, and completed documents. This does not limit our recordkeeping or file-related obligations under applicable law and professional rules.

12. Privacy Requests

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to opt out of certain processing.

To submit a request, email info@thirulegal.com with the subject “Privacy Request,” call 832-500-5171, or write to our address below.

We may verify your identity before responding. Requests remain subject to applicable exceptions, including recordkeeping requirements, professional obligations, and the rights of others.

If we decline a request, you may ask us to reconsider by emailing info@thirulegal.com with the subject “Privacy Appeal.” We will review your appeal and respond within any applicable legal deadline. Where applicable, you may also submit a complaint to the Texas Attorney General.

13. Children’s Privacy

Our website is intended for adults seeking information or assistance. We do not knowingly collect information directly through the website from children under 13.

Parents, guardians, or authorized representatives may provide information about children when relevant to requested services. Contact us if you believe a child has submitted information directly.

14. International Processing

Information may be accessed, processed, or stored in the United States and other countries where our authorized personnel and service providers operate. Privacy laws in those countries may differ from those in your location.

15. External Websites

Our website may link to government websites, payment platforms, and other external services. This policy does not govern those websites. Review their policies before providing information.

16. Policy Updates

We may update this policy as our practices, website, or legal obligations change. Updates will appear on this page with a revised effective date. We will provide additional notice where required.
