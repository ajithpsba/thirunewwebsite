U.S. Immigration Law

Immigration counsel for individuals, families, employers, and investors.

`

Based in Houston, Thiru Legal PLLC handles federal immigration matters nationwide and assists clients applying for U.S. visas through embassies and consulates abroad. Whether you're petitioning for a family member, transferring an employee to the United States, or responding to a government notice, we help you understand your options, prepare your evidence, and decide how to proceed.

Family Immigration & Green Cards

We help U.S. citizens and lawful permanent residents petition for eligible relatives. We review the family relationship, immigration history, and supporting documents to identify the appropriate process and address issues that may affect the case.

  • Marriage-based green cards. Prepare petitions and applications, organize evidence of the relationship, and help couples prepare for interviews.
  • Other family petitions. Assist with petitions for eligible children, parents, and siblings.
  • Fiancé visas. Prepare K-1 petitions and assist with the subsequent green card application.
  • Adjustment of status. Prepare green card applications for eligible applicants in the United States and advise on related work authorization and travel applications.
  • Consular processing. Help families pursue immigrant visas abroad, including at the National Visa Center and U.S. embassies and consulates.
  • Affidavits of support. Review sponsor income and assets, joint sponsor options, and supporting financial records.
  • Removal of conditions. Prepare joint petitions and evaluate waiver options when a joint filing is unavailable or inappropriate.

Citizenship & Naturalization

We help permanent residents apply for naturalization and evaluate whether someone may already have acquired or derived U.S. citizenship through a parent.

  • Eligibility review. Review residence, travel, marital history, and prior immigration filings. Assess issues such as extended absences or arrests that may require closer attention before filing.
  • Application and interview preparation. Prepare naturalization applications and help clients prepare for the interview and applicable testing.
  • Citizenship through parents. Evaluate potential citizenship claims and help gather records to support them.

Consular Processing & NVC

We help clients with immigrant and nonimmigrant visa matters abroad, including case processing with the National Visa Center (NVC) and visa applications at U.S. embassies and consulates.

  • National Visa Center processing. Assist with the DS-260 immigrant visa application, civil documents, financial sponsorship evidence where required, and responses to NVC requests for additional information.
  • U.S. embassy and consulate applications. Assist with supporting documents for family, employment, investor, and business visitor visa cases. Review the DS-160 for nonimmigrant visa applications where applicable.
  • NVC and consular correspondence. Prepare case inquiries, address document or processing issues, and communicate with the NVC or consular post as appropriate.
  • Visa interview preparation. Review the application and supporting evidence with you, discuss likely questions, and help you prepare to explain the facts of your case clearly and accurately.
  • Post-interview follow-up. Review consular notices, help respond to requests for additional documents, and assist with follow-up inquiries when a case is in administrative processing.

Business & Employment Immigration

We advise employers, multinational companies, executives, managers, professionals, and performers on temporary work classifications and employment-based permanent residence. We help with initial filings and review how changes in employment, duties, or work location may affect an existing case.

  • L-1A and L-1B intracompany transfers. Assist with transfers of executives, managers, and employees with specialized knowledge, including new-office petitions, extensions, Blanket L matters, and related applications for family members.
  • H-1B specialty occupations. Assist with initial petitions, changes of employer, extensions, and amendments. Review proposed changes in duties or work location.
  • O-1 extraordinary ability or achievement. Review professional accomplishments, identify supporting evidence, and prepare petitions.
  • TN USMCA professionals. Assist eligible Canadian and Mexican professionals and their U.S. employers with documentation of the proposed position, professional credentials, and employer support.
  • P visas for athletes, entertainers, and performers. Assist with P-1, P-2, and P-3 petitions and related petitions for eligible essential support personnel.
  • Business visitor visas. Review proposed U.S. business activities and assist with supporting documents and consular interview preparation.
  • EB-1A extraordinary ability. Review the applicant's accomplishments, identify supporting evidence, and prepare the petition.
  • EB-1C multinational executives and managers. Review the relationship between the U.S. and foreign companies, the applicant's roles, and how each business operates.
  • EB-2 National Interest Waiver. Help professionals and entrepreneurs describe their proposed U.S. work and document their qualifications, track record, and plans.
  • EB-3 employer-sponsored green cards. Assist employers sponsoring skilled workers, professionals, and other workers. Review the position and the worker's qualifications, prepare the Form I-140 petition, and coordinate next steps toward permanent residence.
  • PERM permanent labor certification. Assist employers with defining job requirements, requesting a prevailing wage determination, planning and documenting recruitment, and preparing the labor certification application when required.
  • Permanent residence processing. Coordinate the immigrant petition with adjustment of status or consular processing, including related applications for eligible family members.

Investor & Entrepreneur Immigration

We help investors, entrepreneurs, and international companies evaluate immigration options tied to U.S. trade, investment, and expansion. Reviewing the plan early gives you time to address questions about ownership, funding, staffing, and operations before committing to a transaction.

  • E-1 Treaty Trader. Prepare applications for treaty traders and qualifying employees using trade, ownership, and business records.
  • E-2 Treaty Investor. Assist investors and qualifying employees with applications involving a new or acquired U.S. business. Review ownership, investment records, business operations, and the proposed role.
  • EB-5 Immigrant Investor. Assist with investor petitions, source and path of funds documentation, and petitions to remove conditions on permanent residence.
  • U.S. expansion for foreign companies. Evaluate options for opening a U.S. office and transferring key personnel. Identify corporate, staffing, and operational records that can support the case.

Immigration Court & Removal Defense

We represent people in removal proceedings, including clients in immigration detention and clients whose cases continue after release. We review the charging documents, immigration history, prior filings, and court orders to assess the options available.

  • Immigration Court hearings. Prepare clients and witnesses, gather evidence, and represent clients at master calendar and individual merits hearings.
  • Asylum and related protection. Develop claims for asylum, withholding of removal, and protection under the Convention Against Torture.
  • Cancellation of removal. Assess eligibility and prepare evidence about residence, family circumstances, hardship, and other relevant facts.
  • Detention and release requests. Assess whether bond proceedings or other release options are available and prepare supporting materials as appropriate.
  • Existing removal orders. Review prior decisions, procedural history, and available records to evaluate possible next steps.

If a family member is detained, include the detention location, if known, and any upcoming hearing date when requesting a consultation.

Humanitarian Immigration

We assist individuals seeking immigration protection or relief based on their circumstances. We help clients prepare declarations, gather supporting records, and present the facts of their case.

  • Asylum. Assist with applications before U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services (USCIS) or in Immigration Court, depending on the case.
  • VAWA self-petitions. Assist eligible survivors of abuse with petitions and supporting evidence.
  • U visa matters. Assist eligible victims of qualifying crimes with petitions and related documentation.
  • Other humanitarian requests. Evaluate select requests based on the circumstances, available evidence, and applicable agency process.

Waivers, Visa Refusals & Entry Issues

We review prior visa refusals, overstays, removal orders, and problems at U.S. ports of entry. We help you understand how those events may affect a future application or travel plan.

  • Waivers. Evaluate immigrant and nonimmigrant waiver options, including provisional unlawful presence waivers where appropriate.
  • Visa refusals. Review refusal notices, prior applications, and interview history to assess whether additional documentation, follow-up with the consular post, or a new application may be appropriate.
  • Port-of-entry issues. Review expedited removal orders, refusals of admission, and other problems arising during border inspection.
  • Form I-94 and admission classification. Assist with select discrepancies involving admission records or the classification recorded at entry.

Requests for Evidence, Denials & Appeals

We assist clients who receive a government notice or an unfavorable decision, including in matters originally filed on their own or with another representative.

  • Requests for Evidence (RFEs). Identify what the agency is asking for and prepare a response supported by relevant records.
  • Notices of Intent to Deny (NOIDs). Review the agency's concerns and prepare a response addressing the facts and evidence.
  • USCIS denials. Evaluate whether a new filing, motion, or administrative appeal is appropriate.
  • Immigration Court decisions. Evaluate and prepare eligible appeals to the Board of Immigration Appeals and motions to reopen or reconsider.

When requesting a consultation about a notice or decision, include its date and any stated response or appeal deadline.

Employer Immigration Compliance

We help employers review their employment verification procedures and assess immigration questions that arise when their workforce or business structure changes.

  • Form I-9 reviews and training. Conduct internal reviews and help staff understand their verification responsibilities.
  • Employment authorization. Advise on documentation and reverification questions.
  • Changes in employment. Review immigration implications when employees change roles, work locations, or employing entities.
  • Business transactions. Assess workforce immigration issues in acquisitions, reorganizations, and changes in ownership.

Immigration Records & FOIA Requests

Prior records can help clarify what was filed, what happened during a border inspection, or what an agency recorded about your case. We identify the agency likely to hold the records, prepare the appropriate request, and review the records released.

  • USCIS FOIA requests. Request an immigration file, also known as an A-File, or specific records such as prior applications, petitions, notices, and decisions.
  • CBP FOIA requests. Request available travel history, port-of-entry inspection records, Border Patrol encounter records, and other records held by U.S. Customs and Border Protection.
  • ICE records requests. Seek available detention, enforcement, or removal records held by U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement.
  • Immigration Court records. Help obtain the Record of Proceedings or prior decisions through the appropriate Executive Office for Immigration Review process.
  • Review of released records. Compare the records with your immigration history, identify gaps or inconsistencies, and assess how the information may affect a pending or future matter.
